Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026
App: SnapMind (package app.snapmind)
Publisher: Thanapat Wongchalermthan

SnapMind reads screenshots you allow, on your phone, so you can search the words on them. It does not upload those screenshots, require an account, or send the text to a SnapMind server.

This Privacy Policy describes how Thanapat Wongchalermthan (“we”, “us”) handles information in the Android app SnapMind. It applies only to this app.

1. Who we are

SnapMind is a private index for screenshots already on your phone. It files each screenshot and lets you search the words in the image. For privacy questions, contact pat23.w@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

SnapMind does not operate a cloud account. The search index is stored only in the app’s private storage on this phone. The screenshot files stay in your gallery (Android MediaStore) or at the location you picked. SnapMind keeps a reference to each image so it can show it again. It does not copy a second photo library.

For a screenshot you allow the app to read, the local index can include:

  • A reference to the image (its content address and file name) and when it was added
  • Text recognized on the image, used for search
  • A category, title, amount, date, and place read from that text
  • The contents of a QR code or barcode, when one is present
  • Whether you marked the screenshot as a wishlist item
  • The status of on-device categorization described in section 4

Password, passcode, and one-time-code screens are treated as private. SnapMind stores a hidden placeholder instead of the text on those screens. That text is not written into the database and is not searchable.

3. Information we do not collect ourselves

We do not run a backend that receives your screenshots, recognized text, categories, amounts, QR contents, or wishlist marks. We do not sell your personal information. There is no SnapMind account. The app does not include our own advertising or analytics SDK.

Installing SnapMind from Google Play is a transaction with Google. Google’s own privacy policy applies to your Play account, purchases, and store activity. That is separate from the index SnapMind keeps on your phone.

4. Processing on your phone

SnapMind reads text and barcodes on the device. Category, title, amount, date, and place are filled in on the device from that text.

On a phone that already includes Gemini Nano, SnapMind may ask that on-device model once to suggest a category when the built-in rules do not recognize the screenshot. The text used for that step stays on the phone. SnapMind does not send it to a SnapMind server or to a cloud AI service. A phone without that model still keeps a searchable record.

A QR or barcode payload is saved in the local index. It opens in another app only after you confirm. The app you open then handles that link under its own policy.

5. Permissions

Depending on your Android version and what you allow, SnapMind may request:

  • Photos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES on Android 13 and newer) so it can watch your Screenshots folder and index new screenshots
  • On Android 12 and below, storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) for the same purpose
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) so a reminder you set can appear

Broad photo access is used to index new screenshots as you take them. The system photo picker selects images once and cannot watch the Screenshots folder. If you decline broad access, you can pick individual screenshots instead, and SnapMind indexes only those. You can revoke photo or notification access in Android settings.

SnapMind does not request internet access in order to build this index.

6. Reminders

If a flight or ticket screenshot has a clear future date, you can tap “Remind me a day before.” The reminder is scheduled on this phone. The notification shows the screenshot title. SnapMind does not send that reminder through a server. Nothing is scheduled until you tap.

7. Device backups

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this app’s private database in a device backup. That backup is controlled by your device and your Google account backup settings. SnapMind does not run its own cloud copy of the index. Hidden sign-in text is not in the database, so it is not part of that backup.

Screenshots in your gallery follow your device’s photo backup settings, which are outside this app.

8. Data retention and deletion

The local index remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the index from the device, unless a device backup you keep still holds a copy. Screenshots stay in your gallery until you delete them there.

Because we do not hold a copy of your screenshots or index on our servers, there is no SnapMind-side dataset to delete. For questions, email pat23.w@gmail.com.

9. Children’s privacy

SnapMind is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a server, because the app does not send the index to us.

10. International users

The index is created and kept on your phone. We do not transfer your screenshot text to our own servers in any country. Device backup, if you turn it on, is handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when we do. The current version is published at https://immrpat.github.io/snapmind-privacy-policy/.
